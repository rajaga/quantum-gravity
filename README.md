# quantum-gravity
A proposal on quantum gravity by Venkatesa Chandrasekaran has been published on arxiv - "An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone" (arXiv:2611.34924). 

The repo is an amateur's attempt to use AI to contribute towards this approach. Perhaps the signal to noise ratio is alright.

Critiques of the approach have also started appearing. For example, "Has AI come for quantum gravity" by Jonathan Oppenheim. The document "A Critical Revision and Semiclassical Reformulation of Null-Cone
Algebraic Gravity" is in fact an attempt to answer some of his objections. I hope that it does not fall under the 'not even wrong' category.
