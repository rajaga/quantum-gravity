# NCG-006: Reduced Phase Space vs. Dirac Quantization and QRF Clock-Switching

## 1. Claim Being Examined

In Sections 3--5 and Appendix A.3 of `arXiv:2611.34924v1` (Chandrasekaran, 2026) [1], classical constraints along intersecting null cones—most notably the null Raychaudhuri equation $\mathfrak{C}_l = 0$ on expanding null generators ($\partial_u \rho > 0$)—are solved classically to construct a reduced phase space and a gravitational dressing clock $\hat{\bar{u}}_*$ [2] prior to quantizing the remaining radiative and corner edge-mode degrees of freedom.

Expanding Section 4 of `NCG-000`, this note audits the structural gap between **Reduced Phase Space Quantization (RPSQ)** and **Dirac quantization** [3], derives the exact operator-ordering and spectral shifts produced by classical constraint rescaling, constructs the branch-resolved Refined Algebraic Quantization (RAQ) [6] and Quantum Reference Frame (QRF) / Page--Wootters clock-switch map [7] in a solvable quadratic model, and isolates the specific ultraviolet and anomaly obstructions that prevent an immediate lift of this resolution to four-dimensional null-cone gravity.

## 2. Verdict and Summary

**Unconditional toy-model results:**

1. **Linear constraint equivalence requires specific ordering and inner product:** For the linear constraint $C_{\mathrm{lin}} = u p_u + H_{\mathrm{mat}}(q, p) = 0$ on $u > 0$, RPSQ in the clock $T = \ln u$ matches Dirac quantization on $L^2(\mathbb{R}_+, du) \otimes L^2(\mathbb{R}, dq)$ only after choosing the symmetric Weyl ordering $\hat{p}_T = \frac{1}{2}(\hat{u}\hat{p}_u + \hat{p}_u\hat{u}) = -i\hbar(u\partial_u + \frac{1}{2})$ (which is essentially self-adjoint on $C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}_+)$, unlike $\hat{p}_u = -i\hbar\partial_u$) and defining the physical inner product via group averaging with $\delta(\hat{C}_{\mathrm{lin}})$.
2. **Constraint rescaling creates a physical $-\hbar^2/4$ spectral shift:** For the quadratic constraint $C_{\mathrm{quad}} = p_u^2 - u^{-2} p_q^2 = 0$, classical rescaling by $u^2$ yields $\tilde{C} = p_T^2 - p_q^2 = 0$. On $C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}_+)$, the symmetric orderings obey $\hat{u}\hat{p}_u^2\hat{u} = \hat{p}_u\hat{u}^2\hat{p}_u = \hat{p}_T^2 + \frac{\hbar^2}{4} \ge \frac{\hbar^2}{4}$ (by Hardy's inequality). Quantizing the constraint as $\hat{\tilde{C}}_1 = \hat{p}_T^2 - \hat{p}_q^2$ yields a gapless spectrum ($p_q \in \mathbb{R}$), whereas $\hat{\tilde{C}}_2 = \hat{u}\hat{p}_u^2\hat{u} - \hat{p}_q^2$ opens a quantum momentum threshold $\lvert p_q \rvert \ge \hbar/2$.
3. **Single-branch RPSQs in different clocks do not inhabit the same physical sector:** On the constraint surface $p_T^2 - p_q^2 = 0$ (four momentum rays), fixing the positive-frequency branch in Clock $T$ ($p_T = +\lvert p_q \rvert$) keeps Rays I and II, whereas fixing the positive branch in Clock $q$ ($p_q = +\lvert p_T \rvert$) keeps Rays I and IV—an explicit realization of the multiple-choice problem [4, 5]. Only the full two-branch Dirac/RAQ Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{phys}} = \mathcal{H}_{\chi=+} \oplus \mathcal{H}_{\chi=-}$ supports a globally unitary Page--Wootters clock-switch map $\hat{S}_{T \to q}$ [7].

**Conditional implication for `arXiv:2611.34924v1`:**

Solving $\mathfrak{C}_l = 0$ classically on $\partial_u \rho > 0$ avoids the ultraviolet renormalization of coincident-point shear-squared operators $(\partial_v \hat{z}^\alpha)(\partial_v \hat{z}_\alpha)$ and the closure of a quantum constraint algebra [8], but permanently excludes off-shell caustic fluctuations ($\partial_u\rho$ crossing zero) and leaves the reduced quantization foliation-, clock-, and state-dependent (as acknowledged in Footnote 20 of [1]).

## 3. Derivation and Audit Steps

### Step 3.1: Linear Constraint and Essential Self-Adjointness of $\hat{p}_T$

Consider a constrained mechanical model with a positive clock coordinate $u \in \mathbb{R}_+ = (0, \infty)$, conjugate momentum $p_u$ ($\{u, p_u\} = 1$), and a matter/graviton degree of freedom $(q, p)$ ($\{q, p\} = 1$). Let the constraint be linear in $p_u$:

$$
C_{\mathrm{lin}} = u p_u + H_{\mathrm{mat}}(q, p) = 0.
$$

In **RPSQ**, changing variables to $T = \ln u \in \mathbb{R}$ gives $p_T = u p_u$, reducing the system to standard Schrödinger evolution on $L^2(\mathbb{R}, dq)$ generated by $H_{\mathrm{phys}} = H_{\mathrm{mat}}(q, p)$ in relational time $T$.

In **Dirac quantization** on $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{kin}} = L^2(\mathbb{R}_+, du) \otimes L^2(\mathbb{R}, dq)$, define the unitary coordinate transformation $U: L^2(\mathbb{R}_+, du) \to L^2(\mathbb{R}, dT)$ by

$$
(U\psi)(T) = e^{T/2}\psi(e^T), \qquad \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \lvert (U\psi)(T) \rvert^2\,dT = \int_0^\infty \lvert \psi(u) \rvert^2\,du.
$$

Under Weyl ordering of the dilation generator,

$$
\hat{p}_T \equiv \frac{1}{2}\left(\hat{u}\hat{p}_u + \hat{p}_u\hat{u}\right) = -i\hbar\left(u\partial_u + \frac{1}{2}\right),
$$

direct evaluation on $\phi(T) = (U\psi)(T)$ (so $\psi(u) = u^{-1/2}\phi(\ln u)$) gives $(U \hat{p}_T U^{-1}\phi)(T) = -i\hbar\partial_T \phi(T)$. Because $-i\hbar\partial_T$ is essentially self-adjoint on $C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R})$, $\hat{p}_T$ is **essentially self-adjoint** on $C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}_+)$. Imposing $\hat{C}_{\mathrm{lin}} = \hat{p}_T + \hat{H}_{\mathrm{mat}}$ via RAQ group averaging $\langle \Psi_1 \lvert \delta(\hat{C}_{\mathrm{lin}}) \rvert \Psi_2 \rangle_{\mathrm{kin}}$ [6] reproduces the RPSQ Hilbert space $L^2(\mathbb{R}, dq)$ and unitary evolution $e^{-i\hat{H}_{\mathrm{mat}} T/\hbar}$.

### Step 3.2: Quadratic Constraint and the $-\hbar^2/4$ Factor-Ordering Shift

Now consider a quadratic constraint analogous to relativistic or gravitational Hamiltonian constraints:

$$
C_{\mathrm{quad}} = p_u^2 - u^{-2} H_{\mathrm{mat}}(q, p) = 0, \qquad u > 0,
$$

specialized to a free matter clock $H_{\mathrm{mat}}(q, p) = p_q^2$. Classically, solving for $p_u$ forces a choice of sign branch $p_u = \pm u^{-1}\lvert p_q \rvert$. Multiplying $C_{\mathrm{quad}}$ classically by $u^2 > 0$ and setting $T = \ln u$ yields

$$
\tilde{C} = u^2 C_{\mathrm{quad}} = (u p_u)^2 - p_q^2 = p_T^2 - p_q^2 = 0.
$$

At the quantum level on $C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}_+)$, different symmetric orderings of $u^2 p_u^2$ differ by $\hbar^2$ constants. Acting on any $\psi \in C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}_+)$:

$$
\hat{p}_T^2 \psi = -\hbar^2\left(u\partial_u + \frac{1}{2}\right)\left(u\psi' + \frac{1}{2}\psi\right) = -\hbar^2\left(u^2\psi'' + 2u\psi' + \frac{1}{4}\psi\right),
$$

whereas both $\hat{u}\hat{p}_u^2\hat{u}$ and $\hat{p}_u\hat{u}^2\hat{p}_u$ act identically as

$$
(\hat{u}\hat{p}_u^2\hat{u})\psi = -\hbar^2 u\frac{d^2}{du^2}(u\psi) = -\hbar^2(u^2\psi'' + 2u\psi') = -\hbar^2\frac{d}{du}(u^2\psi') = (\hat{p}_u\hat{u}^2\hat{p}_u)\psi.
$$

Comparing these two expressions (together with the symmetrized endpoint ordering $\frac{1}{2}(\hat{u}^2\hat{p}_u^2 + \hat{p}_u^2\hat{u}^2)$) yields the exact operator identities on $C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}_+)$:

$$
\hat{p}_T^2 = \hat{u}\hat{p}_u^2\hat{u} - \frac{\hbar^2}{4} = \hat{p}_u\hat{u}^2\hat{p}_u - \frac{\hbar^2}{4}, \qquad \frac{1}{2}\left(\hat{u}^2\hat{p}_u^2 + \hat{p}_u^2\hat{u}^2\right) = \hat{p}_T^2 - \frac{3\hbar^2}{4}.
$$

Under the unitary map $U$, $U(\hat{u}\hat{p}_u^2\hat{u})U^{-1} = -\hbar^2\partial_T^2 + \frac{\hbar^2}{4}$, consistent with Hardy's inequality $\langle \psi \lvert \hat{u}\hat{p}_u^2\hat{u} \rvert \psi \rangle = \hbar^2 \int_0^\infty \lvert (u\psi)' \rvert^2\,du \ge \frac{\hbar^2}{4}\|\psi\|^2$. Consequently, the ordering choice alters the physical spectrum at $O(\hbar^2)$:

| Quantized Constraint | Equivalent $T$-Form | Physical Spectrum ($p_q$) | Dispersion Relation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\hat{\tilde{C}}_1 = \hat{p}_T^2 - \hat{p}_q^2$ | $-\hbar^2\partial_T^2 - \hat{p}_q^2$ | All $p_q \in \mathbb{R}$ (gapless) | $p_T = \pm p_q$ |
| $\hat{\tilde{C}}_2 = \hat{u}\hat{p}_u^2\hat{u} - \hat{p}_q^2$ | $-\hbar^2\partial_T^2 + \frac{\hbar^2}{4} - \hat{p}_q^2$ | $\lvert p_q \rvert \ge \hbar/2$ (gapped) | $p_T = \pm\sqrt{p_q^2 - \hbar^2/4}$ |

### Step 3.3: RAQ Inner Product, the Four-Ray Obstruction, and Unitary Clock-Switching

Fix the gapless self-adjoint quantization $\hat{\tilde{C}}_1 = \hat{p}_T^2 - \hat{p}_q^2$ on $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{kin}} \cong L^2(\mathbb{R}, dT) \otimes L^2(\mathbb{R}, dq)$. In momentum space $(p_T, p_q)$, the constraint locus $p_T^2 - p_q^2 = 0$ (excluding the measure-zero origin) consists of four rays:

* **Ray I:** $p_T = +p_q > 0$ ($\mathrm{sgn}(p_T) = +$, $\mathrm{sgn}(p_q) = +$, chirality $\chi \equiv \mathrm{sgn}(p_T p_q) = +$).
* **Ray II:** $p_T = -p_q > 0$ ($\mathrm{sgn}(p_T) = +$, $\mathrm{sgn}(p_q) = -$, chirality $\chi = -$).
* **Ray III:** $p_T = +p_q < 0$ ($\mathrm{sgn}(p_T) = -$, $\mathrm{sgn}(p_q) = -$, chirality $\chi = +$).
* **Ray IV:** $p_T = -p_q < 0$ ($\mathrm{sgn}(p_T) = -$, $\mathrm{sgn}(p_q) = +$, chirality $\chi = -$).

This immediately exhibits the **multiple-choice problem** [4, 5] for single-branch RPSQ:

* Choosing Clock $T$ and restricting to the positive-frequency branch $p_T = +\lvert p_q \rvert$ retains **Rays I and II**.
* Choosing Clock $q$ and restricting to the positive-frequency branch $p_q = +\lvert p_T \rvert$ retains **Rays I and IV**.

Because their intersection is only Ray I, the two single-branch reduced theories do not describe the same physical sector.

In **RAQ / Dirac quantization** [6], group averaging with $\delta(\hat{\tilde{C}}_1) = \frac{1}{2\lvert\hat{p}_q\rvert}[\delta(\hat{p}_T - \hat{p}_q) + \delta(\hat{p}_T + \hat{p}_q)]$ retains all four rays and defines the physical inner product:

$$
\langle \Phi \mid \Psi \rangle_{\mathrm{phys}} = \langle \Phi \lvert \delta(\hat{p}_T^2 - \hat{p}_q^2) \rvert \Psi \rangle_{\mathrm{kin}} = \int_{\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}} \frac{dp_q}{2\lvert p_q \rvert} \left[ \overline{\Phi(p_q, p_q)}\Psi(p_q, p_q) + \overline{\Phi(-p_q, p_q)}\Psi(-p_q, p_q) \right].
$$

Defining normalized chiral branch wavefunctions $\psi_\chi(p_q) \equiv (2\lvert p_q \rvert)^{-1/2}\Psi(\chi p_q, p_q)$ for $\chi \in \{+, -\}$ yields an isometric identification $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{phys}} = \mathcal{H}_{\chi=+} \oplus \mathcal{H}_{\chi=-} \cong L^2(\mathbb{R}, dp_q) \otimes \mathbb{C}^2$. (Note that $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{phys}}$ can equivalently be split by frequency $\sigma_T = \mathrm{sgn}(p_T)$ or $\sigma_q = \mathrm{sgn}(p_q)$; a single-frequency state of Clock $T$ is a superposition of both frequency sectors of Clock $q$.)

With the measure factor $\sqrt{2\lvert\hat{p}_q\rvert}$ absorbed into the branch-resolved Page--Wootters reduction map [7] so that it is strictly unitary (rather than a raw unnormalized projection $\langle \tau_T \rvert$):

1. **Clock $T$ reduction** $\mathcal{R}_T(\tau): \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{phys}} \to L^2(\mathbb{R}, dq) \otimes \mathbb{C}^2$: Since $\langle T \mid p_T \rangle = (2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2} e^{+i p_T T/\hbar}$, on the branch $\hat{p}_T = \chi \hat{p}_q$ a physical state evolves as
   $$
   \left(\mathcal{R}_T^\chi(\tau)\Psi_{\mathrm{phys}}\right)(p_q) = e^{+i\chi p_q \tau/\hbar}\psi_\chi(p_q), \qquad \left(\mathcal{R}_T^\chi(\tau)\Psi_{\mathrm{phys}}\right)(q) = \left(e^{+\frac{i}{\hbar}\chi\hat{p}_q\tau}\psi_\chi\right)(q) = \psi_\chi(q + \chi\tau),
   $$
   solving $\partial_\tau \psi_\chi^{(T)} = \chi \partial_q \psi_\chi^{(T)}$.
2. **Clock $q$ reduction** $\mathcal{R}_q(q_0): \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{phys}} \to L^2(\mathbb{R}, dT) \otimes \mathbb{C}^2$: Using $p_q = \chi p_T$ and the identical Jacobian $(2\lvert p_q \rvert)^{-1} dp_q = (2\lvert p_T \rvert)^{-1} dp_T$, conditioning on $q = q_0$ yields
   $$
   \left(\mathcal{R}_q^\chi(q_0)\Psi_{\mathrm{phys}}\right)(T) = \psi_\chi(q_0 + \chi T).
   $$
3. **Explicit unitary clock switch** $\hat{S}_{T \to q} = \mathcal{R}_q(q_0) \circ \mathcal{R}_T(\tau)^{-1}: L^2(\mathbb{R}, dq) \otimes \mathbb{C}^2 \to L^2(\mathbb{R}, dT) \otimes \mathbb{C}^2$:
   $$
   \left(\hat{S}_{T \to q} \begin{pmatrix} \phi_+ \\ \phi_- \end{pmatrix}\right)(T) = \begin{pmatrix} \phi_+(T + q_0 - \tau) \\ \phi_-(q_0 + \tau - T) \end{pmatrix}.
   $$

Direct integration verifies $\|\hat{S}_{T \to q}\phi\|_{L^2(dT)\otimes\mathbb{C}^2}^2 = \|\phi\|_{L^2(dq)\otimes\mathbb{C}^2}^2$ identically: on the co-moving branch ($\chi = +1$) the switch is a translation, while on the counter-moving branch ($\chi = -1$) it combines parity reflection with translation.

### Step 3.4: Obstructions to Lifting QRF/Dirac Quantization to 4D Null-Cone Gravity

In `arXiv:2611.34924v1` [1], the classical null Raychaudhuri constraint $\mathfrak{C}_l = 0$ is solved on expanding null generators ($\partial_u \rho > 0$) to express the affine dressing clock $u_*(y)$ as a nonlinear exponential functional of the radiative shear. Lifting the Dirac/QRF resolution above to four-dimensional null-cone gravity—starting from the unreduced symplectic potential in Appendix A.3 (Eq. (A.30a) of [1]) with $(\rho^2, \mu)$ and $(z^\alpha, \pi_\alpha)$ quantized independently—confronts three field-theoretic bottlenecks absent from the toy model:

1. **Coincident-point UV divergences:** The quantum Raychaudhuri operator $\hat{\mathfrak{C}}_v$ contains the nonlinear shear-squared product $\frac{\rho^2}{2} g_{\alpha\beta}(\partial_v \hat{z}^\alpha)(\partial_v \hat{z}^\beta)$. Because the null-cone radiative commutator $[\hat{h}_I(v), \hat{h}_I(v')] \propto i\hbar\,\mathrm{sgn}(v-v')/(vv')$ (Eq. (A.36) of [1]) is distributional and nonlocal along each generator, this composite operator requires covariant renormalization rather than finite-dimensional Weyl ordering.
2. **Factor-ordering and anomaly closure:** Unlike the single $-\hbar^2/4$ c-number shift in Step 3.2, field-theoretic factor ordering of $\hat{\mathfrak{C}}_v$ and the angular constraints $\hat{\mathfrak{C}}_{la}$ across intersecting null cones risks state-dependent anomalies that obstruct closure of the constraint algebra (an active research frontier in null-hypersurface quantization [8]).
3. **Caustic suppression:** Solving $\mathfrak{C}_l = 0$ classically on $\partial_u \rho > 0$ enforces monotonic expansion on every configuration, eliminating the field-theoretic counterpart of branch superpositions and tunneling across caustics ($\partial_u\rho = 0$).

## 4. Assumptions and Failure Modes

* **Self-adjointness on the half-line $\mathbb{R}_+$:** A common pitfall on $L^2(\mathbb{R}_+, du)$ is treating $\hat{p}_u = -i\hbar\partial_u$ as a self-adjoint observable with boundary conditions at $u = 0$. In fact, $\hat{p}_u$ on $C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}_+)$ has unequal deficiency indices $(1, 0)$ and admits **no** self-adjoint extensions at all. By contrast, $\hat{p}_u^2 = -\hbar^2\partial_u^2$ has deficiency indices $(1, 1)$ (admitting a one-parameter family of Robin self-adjoint extensions $\psi'(0^+) = \gamma\psi(0^+)$), while the Weyl-ordered dilation generator $\hat{p}_T = -i\hbar(u\partial_u + \frac{1}{2})$ is essentially self-adjoint without needing boundary conditions at $u = 0$. In the gravitational setting, truncating the inward null cone at a finite spacelike cap $\Sigma_1$ ($\rho = \epsilon\rho_S > 0$) replaces $u = 0$ with an explicit boundary condition whose quantum compatibility with the clock cutoff requires separate verification.
* **Clock-choice dependence of causal-diamond ensembles (`NCG-004`, `NCG-005`):** In Section 5.3 of [1], the black hole entropy trace $Z(l) = \mathrm{Tr}(\mathbf{1}_{[0,l]}(\hat{\bar{u}}_*) e^{-\hat{s}})$ depends directly on the reduced dressing clock $\hat{\bar{u}}_*$ and on the cap truncation $\Sigma_1$. Changing the internal clock or dressing definition alters which states fall below the cutoff $l$ and can modify the subleading logarithmic behavior unless relational clock covariance is established quantum mechanically.
* **Toy-model scope vs. interacting 4D gravity:** Because the quadratic toy model has two degrees of freedom and a free matter sector $H_{\mathrm{mat}} = p_q^2$, its unitary clock switch $\hat{S}_{T \to q}$ demonstrates *what is missing* in single-branch RPSQ—not that an anomaly-free QRF construction already exists for four-dimensional interacting gravity.
